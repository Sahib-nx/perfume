Our Origins & Vision

From Ancient Dunes to Modern Alchemy

Founded in 1976, Diwan Al Attour was born from an unyielding devotion to authentic perfumery. We believe that true luxury lies in patience: the slow maceration of aged agarwood, the dawn harvest of Taif mountain roses, and the nuanced harmony between heritage and contemporary French haute perfumery.

Every fragrance we compose is more than a scent — it is an emotional sanctuary, a silent language that celebrates your individuality with remarkable sillage and uncompromising longevity.

Uncompromising Standards

Our Pillars of Mastery

🌿

Purity of Origin

We exclusively source grade-A natural essences from legendary terroirs: Taif mountain roses, Assam oud, and Grasse jasmine.

⚗️

Artisanal Distillation

Combining traditional copper pot alembic extraction with state-of-the-art cold filtration to preserve delicate aromatic nuances.

👑

Master Blending

Each formula undergoes months of patient maturation and olfactory fine-tuning by master noses before bottling.

✨

Sustainable Luxury

Committed to ethical floral harvests, fair-trade resin sourcing, and recyclable hand-polished crystal flacons.

Our Milestones

A Journey Through Time

1976

The First Atelier

Diwan Al Attour opens its doors as an artisanal boutique dedicated to blending custom bespoke bukhoor and pure oud oils for royalty.

1994

French & Oriental Fusion

We partner with Grasse master perfumers to pioneer the modern Oriental-Floral genre, blending velvety French rose with smoky Arabian incense.

2012

The Imperial Reserve

Launching our private reserve collection featuring 25-year aged wild Cambodian agarwood and Taif rose absolute.

2026

Global Haute Parfumerie

Expanding our flagship boutique presence worldwide with sustainable botanical commitments and bespoke fragrance consultations.
