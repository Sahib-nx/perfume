Expert Advice

The Secrets of Longevity & Sillage

🎯

Where to Apply Fragrance

  • Base of the Throat & Collarbone: Emits fragrance upwards toward your companion with natural body heat.
  • Wrists & Inner Elbows: Movement constantly disperses aromatic molecules into the surrounding air.
  • Behind the Ears & Nape: Creates an enchanting sillage trail wherever you walk without overwhelming.
  • Pro Tip: Apply immediately after bathing onto moisturized skin for 3x longer retention. Never rub wrists together!
✨

The Art of Bespoke Layering

  • Heavy Base + Light Florals: Spray a rich oud base (e.g. Imperial Oud) first, then layer a radiant floral (Yasmina) on top.
  • Citrus + Warm Amber: Combine fresh bergamot top notes with sensual amber for an intoxicating summer dusk aura.
  • Preserve in Cool Darkness: Keep your perfumes away from direct sunlight and humidity to protect delicate natural essences.
