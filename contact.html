Frequently Asked Questions

Client Inquiries

Do you offer international express shipping?
Yes. We provide insured worldwide express shipping with DHL Express. All orders are carefully packed in temperature-controlled luxury presentation gift boxes. Complimentary shipping applies to orders over $150.
Are all ingredients 100% authentic and certified?
Every perfume of Diwan Al Attour is crafted exclusively with certified grade-A botanical distillates, pure Taif rose absolute, natural ambergris, and ethically harvested wild agarwood. Each flacon includes a Certificate of Authenticity.
Can I book a private bespoke fragrance blending session?
Yes. Our master noses host private bespoke consultations at our Dubai, Riyadh, and Paris flagship ateliers. You can request a session through the form above or by contacting our WhatsApp VIP concierge.
What is your satisfaction & return policy?
We offer a 5-day return and exchange policy. Every full-sized bottle comes with a complimentary matching sample vial, allowing you to test the scent on your skin before opening the main crystal flacon seal.
